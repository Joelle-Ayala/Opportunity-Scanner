# Opportunity Scanner Demo Video Runbook

Last updated: August 3, 2026

## Demo Goal

Show one clear transformation: Opportunity Scanner turns a company website into sourced public-sector opportunities, then gives the user a target, Revenue Motion, contact path, and next action.

Primary CTA: **Run a free scan at opportunityscanner.ai.**

Use **CivicStage Talent Network**, the fictional company already used in the public sample report, for public recordings. Keep SchoolGig and Reparel as internal regression accounts unless written permission allows their names, logos, and report data to appear publicly.

## Recording Readiness Gate

Do not record until every item passes on the live production release:

- CivicStage is attached to a time-bounded, full-access demo account and opens without a paywall.
- The dashboard has one ready report, one saved search, one active monitor, and one seeded pursuit with a realistic next step.
- The report passes quality review: relevant rows, working official source links, honest `Live opportunities` and `Funded-buyer evidence` sections, and no past date labeled as a deadline.
- At least one strong row has a buyer or partner target, Revenue Motion, contact path, next best action, CRM-ready note, and outreach angle.
- Report, opportunity detail, pursuit, export, comparison, and alert screens work at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844.
- No loading, empty, error, beta-debug, admin, billing problem, or checkout-return states appear.
- Browser zoom is 100%; notifications, password managers, bookmarks, and personal browser profiles are hidden.
- The recording account contains no real customer data, personal contact enrichment, fabricated credits, Stripe history, or unrelated reports.

Record the form and completed result as separate takes. Do not wait for a live scan during the final video.

- Use the 16:9 master in the homepage product-proof section with captions enabled by default and sound off until the visitor opts in.
- Poster frame: the strongest opportunity row with `Source -> Target -> Motion -> Action` visible.
- Link the end card and nearby button to `/#scan` with video-specific UTM parameters.
- Provide a text transcript below or adjacent to the player for accessibility and search discovery.

## Capture Standards

- Record 1440 x 900 or 1920 x 1080 at 30 fps; keep the pointer visible and deliberate.
- Use 125-150% page zoom only for a planned detail insert, never mid-shot.
- Keep each interaction in a separate take with two seconds of stillness before and after the action.
- Capture a clean desktop master first, then separate 9:16 detail shots; do not crop dense desktop tables into unreadable vertical video.
- Use the Opportunity Scanner logo, teal accent, product UI, and existing social-profile watermark. Avoid stock imagery and synthetic customer logos.
- Burn in high-contrast captions, keep important UI out of platform safe zones, and export a caption file plus transcript.

## Redaction And Privacy Checklist

- Hide email addresses, magic links, account IDs, scan IDs, report URLs with private parameters, and browser profile names.
- Hide Stripe customer data, billing portal screens, card details, receipts, webhook data, and subscription identifiers.
- Hide personal contact enrichment, private CRM records, internal notes, admin screens, raw connector responses, scoring/debug data, and environment values.
- Do not show SchoolGig, Reparel, Jammcard, or another real company as a customer, endorsement, or case study without written permission.
- Verify every visible source record immediately before recording; show its year and current/evidence class honestly.
- Never say a historical award is open, closing soon, or available to apply for.
- Replace any accidental personal data with a new clean take; do not rely on blur for the master recording.

## Assumptions

- CivicStage remains the approved fictional public demo identity and its sample records remain source-faithful.
- The authenticated demo entitlement provides full report and monitoring access but does not fabricate contact-enrichment credits or Stripe billing state.
- The $49 Report is the only price stated unconditionally; Monitor and Growth are shown only when their production checkout and operating readiness are verified.
- A dedicated Opportunity Scanner-owned demo login is preferable long term; until then, any approved internal login must be fully redacted from the recording.
