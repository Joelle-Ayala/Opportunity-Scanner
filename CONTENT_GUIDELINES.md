# Opportunity Scanner Content Guidelines

These rules apply to every new Opportunity Scanner resource article.

## Evidence standard

- Every new article must contain at least one real datapoint from a scan or a public API.
- An article without a real datapoint must be explicitly labeled as a framework piece.
- Historical awards are funded-buyer evidence. They must include the award year and must never use deadline, closing-soon, or other urgency framing.
- Only verified live records with a future close date may be described as current opportunities or given deadline language.
- Scanned companies stay anonymized as specified in the curated data. Public award recipients and amounts may be cited as public record.

## Required article elements

- A quick-answer box is mandatory.
- Hero and social images must be product screenshots or data graphics. Never use stock imagery.
- Every article must include a source-backed scan call to action.
- Draft one social-post seed per article at the bottom of the content as an HTML comment.

Use this format for the social seed:

```html
<!-- Social post seed: [One evidence-led insight written as a standalone post.] -->
```

## Freshness and wording

- Every dollar figure from historical award evidence must carry its award year.
- Before calling a record current, verify that its close date is later than the render or publish date.
- Re-verify content labeled current or tied to a calendar year during the quarterly content refresh.
- If a current record becomes stale, remove it or reframe it as historical funded-buyer evidence.
- Evidence-class language uses past tense, such as "received." It must not say "is offering," "is hiring," or "closes soon."

## Editorial review checklist

- The article contains at least one real datapoint or is visibly labeled as a framework piece.
- Historical and current records are clearly separated.
- Every amount includes its year and source.
- The quick-answer box is present.
- The hero and social preview use product or data proof.
- The scan CTA is present.
- The social-post seed is included in an HTML comment.
