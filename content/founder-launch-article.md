# Why I Built Opportunity Scanner: The Opportunities Were Public, but the Path Forward Wasn't

I kept encountering the same business-development problem in markets that looked unrelated on the surface. Public funding, procurement, reimbursement, workforce programs, and policy could create or reveal demand, but the companies positioned to benefit often did not describe themselves in the language those systems used.

The information was public. The path from a public record to a practical business decision was not.

## The same translation problem kept appearing

Across healthcare, education, workforce, technology, and the creative economy, the details changed but the operating questions were remarkably consistent:

- Does this record describe something we can apply for, or a buyer we should approach?
- Is this a live opportunity, or historical evidence that a budget and buying pathway exist?
- Should we sell to the agency, a funded organization, an award recipient, or a partner?
- Is the correct next move registration, contact research, monitoring, or no pursuit at all?

## Why another database was not enough

Strong public databases already exist. The problem is that each system is organized around its own records and terminology. A founder or revenue team usually starts somewhere else: with a company, an offer, a market, and the question, "Where could we fit?"

Finding a relevant record is only the beginning. A useful answer must explain what the evidence proves, what it does not prove, who the likely target is, which revenue motion fits, and what the team should do next.

That is why Opportunity Scanner starts with the company rather than a contract number, grant identifier, or agency code.

## The product thesis

Opportunity Scanner translates a company's website and stated goals into public-sector search language, checks relevant public sources, and organizes qualified findings around action.

The product separates verified live opportunities from historical funded-buyer evidence. It connects each useful record to a target organization, revenue motion, contact path, and next action. The goal is not a longer report. It is a working opportunity pipeline.

This also means being clear about what the product is not. Opportunity Scanner is not a legal, lobbying, procurement, reimbursement, or grant-writing service. It does not promise eligibility, access, funding, a contract, or an award. AI helps translate and organize the evidence; it should never invent the evidence.

## The standard we are building toward

Every material claim should stay connected to a source. Facts should be separated from recommendations. Historical spending should never be presented as an active solicitation. A personal email should not substitute for the correct pursuit strategy. And when the evidence is too weak, the product should say to research, monitor, or move on.
